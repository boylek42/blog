### Current Moment

I'm scratching my head wondering what should I write about...so I went with the easy option of giving you all a bit of context into my life right now. 

At this moment, it's 21:35 and after doing a bit of reflection, today was a great day. 

* **Sea Dip Catch-up:** I caught up with my friend Sean at noon and we went for a dip in the sea. It was great fun and always so good to catch up with him. He's doing very well for himself, training within EY to become a qualified accountant...he's got brains to burn. 
* **Data Pipeline Work:** After that, I came home and continued working on a data pipeline for work using PySpark in Databricks. This pipeline involves ingesting the output of a Machine Learning Model, serialising all of the rows since the last run into a nested JSON structure, and writing them out to our Data Lake to serve as a REST API payload. The end-goal is to package it all up into a scheduled Lakeflow Job. Really interesting stuff!
* **A Belgian Prank:** After work, I gave my brother Daniel a call. At the moment, he's living in Ghent, Belgium working as a mechanical engineer building wind turbine farms for a company called Jan De Nul. He's a great brother and as you can probably tell, he's crazy smart. Anyways, when I gave him a call he picked up and to my shock it was just a random Belgian guy sitting there. I sat there for a second wondering who this man was and why does he have my brother's phone. Turns out, it was his colleague dropping him home after work. He got me pretty good lol.
* **Startup Dreams:** Once he got back to his apartment, we chatted about how we would both love to create our own companies. In the future, I can see both of us starting a successful business together - I'm just not entirely sure what it will be right now - but I know we will. Not to blow my own whistle, but I think I can come up with some cool business ideas (also a ton of bad ones too 😂). I'm sure in future postings I'll share some of them with you - so stay posted!
* **Exercise:** Daniel put the idea of going for a run into my head. I really love running - so I threw on my gear, stretched (very important!), and did my usual 5K route. I felt terrific after just as I always do. I'm going to run a half-marathon this year. So far, I've run the 2024 Dublin Marathon, 2025 Lake Como Half Marathon and the 2025 Dublin Half Marathon.

My girlfriend Caitlin planned out this really cool holiday (we love travelling) - we'll be travelling to the Picos De Europa national park in Northern Spain and bouncing from town to town hiking. It's my job to get us from Santander to the park, so I'll be driving a rental for two hours on the other side of the road...kinda nervous.
